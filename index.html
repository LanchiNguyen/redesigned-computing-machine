How the work gets made

The same arc, every engagement
Step 01

Listen

Interviews, support tickets, session replays. The problem statement is earned, never assumed — and it's written down before anything is drawn.

Step 02

Frame

One page: who it's for, what changes for them, how we'll know. If the team can't agree on this page, pixels would only hide that.

Step 03

Design

Flows before screens, words before styling. Prototypes go in front of real users weekly — taste sets the bar, evidence settles the arguments.

Step 04

Ship & measure

I stay through build: annotated specs, edge cases, QA passes, and a metrics review four weeks after release. Design isn't done at handoff.
