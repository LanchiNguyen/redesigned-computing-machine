Ledgerly — rebuilding trust in small-business banking

Month-end reconciliation was the most dreaded hour in an owner's week. We turned it into a five-minute ritual they actually keep.

ROLEProduct lead · design
TEAM2 designers · 5 eng · 1 PM
YEAR2024 – 2025
[ hero shot — reconciliation dashboard ]

5 min
MEDIAN MONTH-END
OVERVIEW

Ledgerly is a banking app for owner-operated businesses — cafés, studios, one-van trades. They don't have a finance team; they are the finance team, usually at 11pm. Our job was to make the money side feel handled, not haunted.

PROBLEM

Reconciliation lived in a dense table nobody trusted. Owners exported to spreadsheets, re-checked by hand, and still felt behind. In research, one word came up again and again: dread.

"I don't read it, I brace for it."
— CAFÉ OWNER, DISCOVERY INTERVIEW
41 min median to close a month.
— BASELINE ANALYTICS, N=1,240
PROCESSTHREE MOVES
[ workflow sketches ]
01
Match, don't list
Auto-pair transactions and surface only the handful that need a human eye.
[ prototype flows ]
02
One ritual, weekly
Small weekly nudges instead of a monthly cliff — reconciliation as a habit, not an event.
[ usability tests ]
03
Plain-language money
Every number gets a sentence. No jargon, no red-ink shame, just what happened and what's next.
