Problem

Reconciliation lived in a dense table nobody trusted. Owners exported to spreadsheets, re-checked by hand, and still felt behind. In research, one word kept coming up: dread. The median close took 41 minutes, usually at 11pm.

Approach

We made three moves. Match, don't list: auto-pair transactions and surface only the handful that need a human. One ritual, weekly: small nudges instead of a monthly cliff. Plain-language money: every number gets a sentence, no jargon, no red-ink shame.

“I used to brace for month-end. Now it is five minutes on a Friday and I actually trust the number.” Café owner, post-launch interview
Outcome

Owners now close their month in about five minutes, and for the first time tell us they look forward to it. Activation rose 34%, support load dropped 41%, and NPS moved from 31 to 62 over two quarters.

Design that gets handed over warm.

I am Lana, a product and UX designer working across fintech, B2B, and health. I like the unglamorous middle of a product: the reconciliation screen, the empty state, the flow that everyone avoids until it breaks.

My work usually starts in research and ends in a design system, so what ships stays consistent long after I have moved on. I care about clarity over cleverness, and about leaving teams able to keep building without me.
