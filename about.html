ABOUT

I design the parts of a product people avoid until they break.

Reconciliation screens, empty states, the flow nobody wants to own. I'm a product and UX designer who likes the unglamorous middle of a product, and I try to leave it feeling calm and obvious.

[ portrait ]
STORY

I'm Lana. I started in visual design, drifted into research because I kept wanting to know why a screen wasn't working, and ended up in product design where the two meet. Ten years later I've worked across fintech, B2B tools, and health — mostly on the high-stakes, low-glamour flows where a small confusion costs someone real money or time.

I care about clarity over cleverness. The work I'm proudest of usually looks quiet: a table that finally makes sense, an error message someone can actually act on, a month-end that stopped being dreaded.

APPROACH

I start in research and end in a design system, so what ships stays consistent long after I've moved on. I prototype early, test with real users, and write the interface copy myself — because in most of the products I work on, the words are the UX.
